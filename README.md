# TOTA-Energy-Dashboard

An interactive **R Shiny dashboard hosted by Posit Connect Cloud** that brings together publicly available energy data for the Thompson Okanagan region.

The dashboard was developed by the **Thompson Okanagan Tourism Association (TOTA)** to support energy management reporting for the **UN Tourism International Network of Sustainable Tourism Observatories (INSTO)**. This dashboard contributes to ongoing efforts to better understand the relationship between energy use and tourism in the Thompson Okanagan Region.

## Dashboard

**Live App:** https://tota2-energy-dashboard.share.connect.posit.cloud/

## Features

* EV Charging - location & growth in EV chargers
* Renewable Energy Vs Fossil Fuels - renewable and non-renewable fuel generation
* Transportation - energy demand
